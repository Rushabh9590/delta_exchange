              🛡️ Portfolio Margin (Recommended)
              Active Mode
            

            

              Offsets risk across offsetting options & futures positions. Drastically reduces margin requirements for straddles, spreads, and market making.
            

          


          
          

            

              ⚡ Cross Margin
              Active Mode
            

            

              Shares available wallet margin balance across all open positions. All account equity is used to cushion and prevent liquidation.
            

          


          
          

            

              🔒 Isolated Margin
              Active Mode
            

            

              Assigns a fixed, isolated margin to each individual position. No risk or margin sharing across positions.
